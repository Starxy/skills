A bounded estimator

Abstract

The latent state is not observed. Our estimator reduces error only when the noise is independent; it does not establish causation.

1. Method

We call the hidden variable the latent state. For three trials, set seed=17 and use the bound e≤0.05.

The latent state remains fixed unless the intervention changes it. A 12.5% improvement was observed at 20 ms, but not at 10 ms.1
